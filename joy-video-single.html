Fish Trap Weaver

The Stories of Vietnam

Weaving fish traps is the homeland tradition of my village here in Tat Vien. I’ve been doing this the majority of my 84 years; now I wish to see the next generation carry on the tradition. The standard of living is increasing here and I’m thrilled to see that. I hope to live to 105; the key is to ease your mind, live morally, and pay attention to your health.
