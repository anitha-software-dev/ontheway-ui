Portraits of people we meet on the way.

Reading about a place will teach you facts, but you find out more from conversation. This gallery helps paint a picture of the diversity of lives and experiences in the Buddhist Hindu world. You’ll find snippets of everyday conversations with people, sharing about their life and spirituality, what gives them meaning and brings them joy.

Jesus revealed Himself in the course of ordinary conversations with those He met on the way. When we listen generously, we are able to speak into others’ lives with greater compassion and understanding.
