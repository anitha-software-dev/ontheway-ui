Walk With Others On The Way

On The Way is a chance for the church to engage like never before, and for you to be part of a unique immersive journey. From podcasts and videos to small group sessions and an interactive journal, you’ll learn from cross-cultural workers and get a glimpse of real life in the Buddhist Hindu world.

You may see the work of missions—and your role in it—in a whole new way.
