On The Way

The AGWM Senders fund gives all new appointed general missionaries a boost to their cash budgets to help them share the good news around the world. Now, there are two ways you can support workers who are headed to the Buddhist Hindu world.

Supporting New Workers

People from around the country are responding to the call to live on the way, and many just need a little help to reach their financial goals. Workers like Brian and Megan are eager to take the next step into service, to move their family and begin their work after months of preparation. 

Gifts to the Buddhist Hindu World Sender’s Fund will help new workers who are ready get on the way reach their goals sooner. Your gift will make an impact, whether it’s a one-time donation or monthly support.

Returning to the Field

Over the last 3+ years, the COVID pandemic affected international travel, safety, and everyday life at home and abroad. Now, many cross-cultural workers like Amrita are eager to get back to work after months or years of waiting—some returning to the same area, others changing plans out of necessity and following God’s direction. 

Gifts to the Northern Asia Access Fund will help get workers back in-country and building community on the way. Your gift will make an impact, whether it’s a one-time donation or monthly support.
