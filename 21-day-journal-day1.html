Welcome to the On The Way 21-Day Journey

For the next 21 days, we’ll learn from cross-cultural workers who are on the way with Jesus all over the Buddhist and Hindu world. You’ll walk in their shoes and hear their stories from India to Vietnam, Kathmandu to Dubai.

How to make the most of this journey:

Take it one day at a time. 

  • This is meant to be a journey, and the experience will build over the 21 days.
  • Show up, be in prayer, and be open to and trying something new.
  • Each day includes a short scripture reference related to the practice. It’s best to experience the Word in context. (Even better, discuss with your small group or a friend.) Unless noted, all references are from the English Standard Version (ESV).

If you can, start with each day’s podcast.

  • This will introduce the practice, and give context to questions each day.
  • Each podcast is edited from a longer conversation. Some names and locations have been changed or omitted, for the safety of the workers and their families. And that’s why you won’t see names printed here. 

Stay curious about Buddhist and Hindu people around the world and in your community. 

  • Be open to learning about culture and about individuals.
  • Look for opportunities to meet people near you who come from different backgrounds.
  • Ask God to help you ask questions that will help you understand others. 

Be watchful for your cultural bias.

  • Don’t assume all Buddhist and Hindu people are the same, and be mindful of assumptions you might make based on ethnicity, culture, or religion.
  • It’s natural that you see things through the lens of your culture, but ask God to help you be open to others.

Trust God’s timing and release your expectations for the outcome.

  • We don’t know what’s going on in other people’s lives and hearts, and where your encounters with them fit inside their larger story.
  • God is always at work in people. Our job is to pray for those we encounter, and participate where God is already moving. Trust the Holy Spirit as you walk on the way.

Thinking About the Journey

Do you know anyone from the Buddhist or Hindu world? Or anyone practicing those faiths?

If yes: How did you meet? What have you learned about them and their culture?

If no: What are some assumptions you have? What are you curious about?

Have you personally shared your faith with someone else? If not, what are some reasons why? 

  • Is it lack of opportunity? Fear you might offend someone? Unsure what to say?
