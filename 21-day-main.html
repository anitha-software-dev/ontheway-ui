what it is

The 21-Day Journey

Whether it’s around the world or in your community, the idea of sharing your faith might feel uncomfortable, or intimidating. Especially if it’s with people who might have a different background or beliefs than yours. But what if this could be different? As natural as walking your own path with Jesus, and as authentic as connecting with others who are on the way.

This journey will help you investigate your own thoughts about cross-cultural missions, and your relationship with other people in both your close circles and your broader community. It’s a chance to take the first step on a journey that could change your life—whether it ultimately leads you across the world or into your neighborhood.

the 21-day journey

3 Weeks. 3 Themes.

01

Seeing

Noticing others, staying present, and growing in understanding.

02

Moving

Learning to welcome, show hospitality, and accept others as Christ accepts us.

03

Community

Seeking peace, building authentic friendships, and showing up for others.
